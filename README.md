# Math Lessons

Short interactive lessons for a 9th grader preparing for the PreACT 9, built around the questions missed on a practice test. Each lesson takes 5–10 minutes: read the worked examples, then try the quiz and get feedback right away.

Start with the **[study plan](reference/study-plan.html)**. It ranks the lessons by payoff: questions at stake × how fast the habit can be fixed.

## Lessons, in study-plan order

1. [Finish every section, never leave a blank](lessons/0003-finish-every-section.html): pacing, time caps and guessing (all sections)
2. [Reading: spot the trap answer](lessons/0004-reading-trap-answers.html): answers that are too strong, too narrow or about the wrong paragraph
3. [Science: read the data exactly](lessons/0005-science-read-the-data-exactly.html): the right row, the right units and the passage's own definitions
4. [English: four grammar rules](lessons/0006-english-four-grammar-rules.html): subject–verb agreement, commas before names, dash pairs, semicolons
5. [Science: conflicting viewpoints](lessons/0007-science-conflicting-viewpoints.html): one line per student before answering
6. [English: what's the question's job?](lessons/0008-english-whats-the-questions-job.html): add/delete, conclusions, transitions, sentence placement

### Math

- [The missing-letter rule](lessons/0001-the-missing-letter-rule.html): putting a triangle's sides and angles in order
- [SOH CAH TOA, and when to divide](lessons/0002-soh-cah-toa.html): choosing sin, cos or tan, and whether to multiply or divide

## Read or watch

- [ACT, "Preparing for the ACT Test" 2025–26](https://www.act.org/content/dam/act/unsecured/documents/Preparing-for-the-ACT-e.pdf): official tips for every section, plus a practice test
- [ACT, Preparing for PreACT 9 Secure](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/preact/resources/preparing-for-preact9-secure.html): official PreACT 9 timings and format
- [ACT, Description of the Science Test](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/the-act/test-preparation/description-of-science-test.html): the three Science passage types, including Conflicting Viewpoints
- [Purdue OWL: Transitional Devices](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/mechanics/transitions_and_transitional_devices/transitional_devices.html): transition words grouped by meaning
- [Math Open Reference: side lengths and angles](https://www.mathopenref.com/trianglesideangle.html): drag the corners of a triangle and watch the rule hold
- [Khan Academy: Ordering triangle sides and angles](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/7th-engage-ny/engage-7th-module-6/7th-module-6-topic-b/v/ordering-triangle-sides-and-angles): short video on the same rule
- [Lippman & Rasmussen, §5.5 Right Triangle Trigonometry](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Precalculus/Book:_Precalculus__An_Investigation_of_Functions_(Lippman_and_Rasmussen)/05:_Trigonometric_Functions_of_Angles/5.05:_Right_Triangle_Trigonometry): free open textbook; Example 3 is the "divide" case

## Using the lessons

The lessons are plain HTML files with no build step. Open them from the website or in any browser. They load KaTeX from a CDN to display the math, so they need an internet connection.

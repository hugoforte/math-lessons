How to use this

  1. One lesson per sitting, in order. Each takes 5–10 minutes: read it yourself, then walk your student through the worked examples and have him do the quiz.
  2. Before starting a new lesson, have him redo the previous lesson's quiz without looking. Remembering after a gap is what makes it stick.
  3. Tell me the quiz scores and anything that confused either of you, and I'll adjust the next lesson or write a re-test like the math ones.

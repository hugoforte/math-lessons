1 · Ordering sides & angles

Bigger side ↔ bigger angle across from it.

Across from = missing letter. Side \(YZ\) has no \(X\), so it's across from \(\angle X\).

  1. Match each side to its missing letter.
  2. Rank what you're given. Only two angles? Third \(=180°-\text{the other two}\).
  3. Carry the ranking across.

Example: \(XY=12,\ YZ=8,\ XZ=17\) → \(\angle X<\angle Z<\angle Y\).

2 · SOH CAH TOA

\[\sin=\frac{O}{H}\quad\cos=\frac{A}{H}\quad\tan=\frac{O}{A}\]

  1. Find the given angle (not the \(90°\)).
  2. Label: H across from the \(90°\); O across from the angle; A touches the angle.
  3. Know + want: O&H → sin · A&H → cos · O&A → tan.
  4. Unknown on top → multiply. On bottom → divide.
  5. Check: H is the longest side.

Ramp: know O = 3, want H → \(\sin5°=\frac{3}{L}\) → \(L=\frac{3}{\sin5°}\).
Ladder: know H = 20, want A → \(\cos70°=\frac{d}{20}\) → \(d=20\cos70°\).

Trap: \(3\sin5°\) is smaller than 3, so it can't be a hypotenuse.
